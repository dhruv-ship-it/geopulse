what a one-ring neighbourhood means on the ground, over 1200 zones in a 200 km square
  two zones 9.2 km apart can already be non-neighbours
  two zones 31.7 km apart can still be neighbours
  so cell adjacency brackets a distance threshold rather than equalling one. Closer than
